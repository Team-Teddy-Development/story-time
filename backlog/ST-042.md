## Story
As a story-time maintainer, I want push to reconcile a story that still has pending_create after it is moved to done, split, or archived, so that an issue made by a lost create does not stay open and unlinked.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Given a story with pending_create and no remote whose status is done, split, or archived, and an open tracker item titled with its ID, when story push runs, then it links that item and applies the story's closed state to it, verified by a native/story-push test with a fake tracker
- [x] Given the same story and no matching tracker item, when story push runs, then it skips the story and removes nothing

## Out of Scope
Reconciliation of operations other than create.

## Notes
Source: ST-038 review. native/story-push/src/lib.rs:457-466 plans a closed story with no remote as skip, so a pending_create marker on it is never reconciled. Affected command: story push.

## Test Plan
Red: a fake-tracker test with a closed story carrying pending_create and an existing open item. Verify with cargo test --locked in native/story-push.

## Evidence
- Red 3d2f0a7: `closed_story_with_a_lost_create_links_and_closes_the_open_item` (tests/closed_pending_create.rs) failed: `pushed[0].action` was null. It runs the real binary against a stateful fake Gitea for done, split, and archived.
