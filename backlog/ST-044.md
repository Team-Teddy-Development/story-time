## Story
As a maintainer adopting an existing project, I want adoption preview to accept a legacy story with prose after the frontmatter and before any section, so that plausible legacy backlogs are not rejected as unsupported.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Given a legacy story with free prose between the frontmatter and its first ## section, when story adopt preview runs, then the story is reported as preserved, not unsupported_story, and its bytes are unchanged after apply, verified by a native/story-push test

## Out of Scope
Changing how story-time renders or edits its own stories.

## Notes
Source: ST-023 review. native/story-push/src/story.rs:84 rejects any non-blank line before the first `## ` section ("Content outside a named story section is unsupported"), so adoption preview reports such a legacy story as `unsupported_story` and apply cannot run. The ST-023 AC5 fixture worked around it by moving prose under `## Notes`. Affected command: story adopt preview/apply.

## Test Plan
Red: an adoption_contract test with a legacy story that has prose before its first section. Verify with cargo test --locked in native/story-push.

## Evidence
- Red df29cf9: `legacy_story_with_prose_before_its_first_section_is_preserved_byte_for_byte` (tests/adoption_contract.rs) failed with diagnostic `unsupported_story` for work/legacy.md.
- Green 0da58f5: adoption reads stories with `story::parse_legacy`, which skips prose before the first section. Preview reports status preview with no diagnostics, and the file bytes are unchanged after apply. Push and planning keep the strict `parse`; `story::tests::rejects_lossy_input` still passes.
