## Story
As a project stakeholder, I want a project-status skill, so that I can get a dated status report in which every claim links to its record.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given goals, sprint and flow reports, metrics, and risks, when the project-status skill runs, then it writes a dated report that separates accepted, released, blocked, and forecast work, names risks, decisions, owners, and next actions, and links every status claim to an authoritative record, verified by cargo test --test project_status_skill on skills/project-status/SKILL.md

## Out of Scope
Other lifecycle skills. Changing refinement, sprint-planning, or story-accept. Value scores and dates.
