## Story
As a project stakeholder, I want a project-status skill, so that I can get a dated status report in which every claim links to its record.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Given goals, sprint and flow reports, metrics, and risks, when the project-status skill runs, then it writes a dated report that separates accepted, released, blocked, and forecast work, names risks, decisions, owners, and next actions, and links every status claim to an authoritative record, verified by cargo test --test project_status_skill on skills/project-status/SKILL.md

## Out of Scope
Other lifecycle skills. Changing refinement, sprint-planning, or story-accept. Value scores and dates.

## Notes
Source: ST-032 (epic EP-002, map backlog/EP-002.map.md). Slice 4: trusted reporting. Responsibility: docs/spikes/ST-019-atlassian-lifecycle.md (line 122).
