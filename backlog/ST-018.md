## Story

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given open stories and missing epic definitions, when refinement runs, then every open story has a disposition and evidence-based epic mapping or an explicit unresolved question.
- [ ] Given ready stories and team capacity, when sprint planning runs, then the plan records a sprint goal, capacity arithmetic, dependency order, and deferred work without treating future forecasts as commitments.
- [ ] Given missing capacity or unsupported tracker sprint fields, when planning runs, then it records a provisional plan and publication limits without inventing dates, metrics, or CLI commands.
- [ ] Given the skill package, when its validation and installation paths run, then both new skills and their router entries are discoverable with valid metadata.
