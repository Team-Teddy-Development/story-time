## Story
As a story-time maintainer, I want two pushes that edit one issue's labels at the same time to keep both changes, so that neither push overwrites the other's labels.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Given two story push runs that change different labels on one issue at the same time, when both finish, then the issue carries both changes, verified by a native/story-push test with a fake tracker

## Out of Scope
Locking fields other than labels. Ambiguous creates (ST-038).

## Notes
Source: gap-sweep row 5 on 8f65a07. ST-004 Out of Scope: concurrent label-editor locking. Affected command: story push.

## Test Plan
Red: a fake tracker test with two interleaved label edits. Verify with cargo test --locked in native/story-push.
