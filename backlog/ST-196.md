## Story
As a team lead, I want a completion forecast with a range, such as "50% by Dec 3, 85% by Dec 9", for the rest of a sprint, an epic or a set of stories, so that the dates I commit to reflect how we actually deliver rather than points divided by an average.

## Source task
2026-11-25, velocity and burndown design (PR #444). Depends on ST-194. ST-198 later calibrates its samples with the estimated-against-shipped history of ST-193. Eric, 2026-11-25: the goal is better forecasting on the delivery dashboard, so the JSON is shaped for it.

## Acceptance Criteria
Fixture for every criterion: settings `team.sprints` gives two-week sprints `2026-S01` to `2026-S07`, with `2026-S05` from 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-15, `2026-S06` from 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-29 and `2026-S07` from 2026-11-30 to 2026-12-13. Code in `native/story-push/src/forecast.rs`, tests in `native/story-push/tests/forecast.rs`.

- [ ] Given closed sprints S01 to S05 with velocity 10 each and stories A and B of size 10 each not done, when `story forecast --ids A,B --today 2026-11-15 --json` runs, then `rows` equals `[{"percentile": 50, "date": "2026-12-13"}, {"percentile": 85, "date": "2026-12-13"}]`
- [ ] Given closed sprints S01 to S05 with velocities 8, 10, 12, 9 and 11 and 20 remaining points, when `story forecast --ids A,B --today 2026-11-15 --seed 7 --json` runs twice, then the two outputs are byte-identical
- [ ] Given story A of size 5 and story B with no size, both not done, when `story forecast --ids A,B --today 2026-11-15 --json` runs, then `remaining_points` is 5 and `unknown` equals `[{"id": "B", "reason": "not sized"}]`
- [ ] Given sprint `2026-S06` with 12 committed points, 4 of them done and 2 points dropped to `2026-S07`, when `story forecast --sprint 2026-S06 --today 2026-11-20 --json` runs, then `remaining_points` is 6

## Notes
Decisions:
- **Targets.** `--sprint ID` forecasts the sprint's remaining scope from ST-194's derivation (committed plus adds, minus drops, minus done). `--epic ID` and `--ids A,B` forecast the open stories they name.
- **Samples.** Each run draws one closed-sprint velocity per future sprint, with replacement. The sprint holding `--today` contributes its draw times the share of its calendar days after `--today`, so a forecast made on a sprint's last day starts with the next sprint. Velocity is ST-194's completed points per closed sprint. With fewer than 3 closed sprints both dates are `unknown` with the count, as ST-194's velocity is; ST-204 adds the daily-throughput fallback (the design's "daily throughput when there are fewer than 3 sprints").
- **Split.** Poker sized the whole forecast 8/8/5 (two sample sources, three targets, the unknown paths). The daily-throughput fallback and its no-history path moved to ST-204, which depends on this story. Repositories with no sprints, such as the ones the delivery dashboard reads today, need ST-204 before the forecast gives them dates.
- **Dates.** A run's date is the end of the sprint, or the day, at which its cumulative draws reach the remaining points. The 50% and 85% dates are the 5,000th and 8,500th of the 10,000 run dates in order (nearest rank).
- **Reproducible.** The random source is SplitMix64, written by hand in about 10 lines. `native/story-push/Cargo.toml` has no random crate (its dependencies are serde, ring, serde_json, reqwest, chrono, regex-lite and tiny_http), and a hand-written generator gives the same sequence on every crate version. The seed defaults to 1 and `--seed` overrides it, so the same history gives the same dates.
- **Ship obligations.** New commands go in `story help` and the native command lists of `AGENTS.md` and `README.md` (the `help_contract` and `doc_native_lists` tests).
- **Done date.** The same source as ST-195: the date of the `set status=done` event, which a merge hook writes as the merge record (ess-monorepo `.github/scripts/ci/story-delivered.js:139-142`; essentials-core `.github/workflows/story-delivered.yml:173`). Velocity is per sprint, so a merge timestamp would not change a sample. ST-201 adds the timestamp for cycle time.
- **Unknown, never zero.** Unsized stories are listed under `unknown` and left out of `remaining_points` (the `metrics.rs` contract, `native/story-push/src/metrics.rs:194-201`). The delivery dashboard counts them as 3 points today.
- **Dashboard shape.** The JSON keeps the `metrics.rs` figure fields (`metric: "forecast"`, `units: "date"`, `window`, `source`, `definition`, `unknown`) and adds `remaining_points`, `sample_source`, `sample_count`, `runs: 10000`, `seed`, and a top-level `rows` array of `{percentile, date}`, which a dashboard dataset loads as rows with no loader. Today the delivery dashboard forecasts one date: open points divided by the points finished in the last 14 days, halved to a weekly pace (its `forecast` calculation). ST-203 replaces that with these two dates.
- **Calibration comes later.** ST-198 adds `--calibrated`, which samples by the ST-193 groups (size by builder by repository, falling back to size by builder, then size). This story samples raw velocity only.
- **MCP and skills moved out.** `story_forecast` and the skill changes moved to ST-197, which owns every MCP tool and skill of the design.
