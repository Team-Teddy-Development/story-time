## Story
As a team lead, I want a completion forecast with a range, such as "50% by Dec 3, 85% by Dec 9", for the rest of a sprint, an epic or a set of stories, so that the dates I commit to reflect how we actually deliver rather than points divided by an average.

## Source task
2026-11-25, velocity and burndown design (PR #444). Depends on ST-194. ST-193 later calibrates its samples with estimated-against-shipped history.

## To plan
- `story forecast (--sprint ID | --epic ID | --ids ...) [--json|--text]`. A seeded Monte Carlo (10,000 runs, reproducible) samples closed-sprint velocity, or daily throughput when there are fewer than 3 sprints.
- Report the 50% and 85% dates, the sample source and count, and the remaining points. Unsized stories are listed as unknown, never counted as zero.
- Read-only MCP tool `story_forecast` (local and hosted).
- The `agile-metrics`, `sprint-planning` and `sprint-review` skills use it: commitment is sized from the velocity range, and the review shows forecast against actual.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given five closed sprints with velocities 8, 10, 12, 9 and 11 and 20 remaining points, when story forecast --json runs with a fixed seed, then it returns the same 50% and 85% dates on every run and the 85% date is not earlier than the 50% date
