## Story
As a product owner, I want a sprint-review skill, so that I can close a sprint honestly with stakeholder feedback and a disposition for unfinished work.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Given a sprint with accepted and unfinished stories and stakeholder observations, when the sprint-review skill runs, then it records the goal outcome, links each piece of feedback to a backlog item, gives every unfinished story an explicit disposition, preserves original estimates and sprint membership, and marks no story done, verified by cargo test --test sprint_review_skill on skills/sprint-review/SKILL.md

## Out of Scope
Other lifecycle skills. Changing refinement, sprint-planning, or story-accept. Value scores and dates.

## Notes
Source: ST-032 (epic EP-002, map backlog/EP-002.map.md). Slice 2: active flow and learning. Responsibility: docs/spikes/ST-019-atlassian-lifecycle.md (line 111). Sprint membership history has no native store yet (spike:195); the skill reads what exists and says what is missing.
