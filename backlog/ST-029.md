## Story
As a story-time maintainer, I want a story created from an open tracker issue to take over that issue, so that the tracker keeps one item per piece of work instead of a duplicate.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given an open tracker issue that no story's remote names, when the story-create skill files a story from it, then the skill keeps the issue's original title and body under Notes and runs story set <id> remote=<n> before its sync step, verified by cargo test --manifest-path native/story-push/Cargo.toml --test gap_sweep_skill

## Out of Scope
Linking or closing a tracker issue that an existing story already covers. Adoption for trackers other than GitHub. Any change to story push.
