## Story
As a story-time operator, I want story-time traces in Tempo and Grafana, so that I can locate slow or failed steps within an operation.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given configured tracing, when a story-time operation runs, then Tempo contains a root span and child spans for the storage or tracker calls it actually performs.
- [ ] Given a failed operation, when its trace opens in Grafana, then span status and sanitized error attributes identify the failed step.
- [ ] Given a trace context, when logs and spans are emitted with logging enabled, then they carry the same trace identifier and Grafana can navigate from the trace to matching Loki logs.
- [ ] Given story bodies or credentials, when spans are exported, then neither appears in span names, attributes, or events.
- [ ] Given disabled tracing or an unavailable backend, when an operation runs, then its result remains correct and sampling, buffering, and export timeouts remain bounded.
