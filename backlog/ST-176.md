## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given an authorized caller, an in-progress GitHub-backed story, its current SHA-256, and one nonempty evidence entry per existing criterion, when the caller submits acceptance, then hosted Story Time opens the reviewed proposal that ticks the criteria without changing their wording, records evidence, marks the story done, and preserves unrelated content. Main and the tracker remain unchanged until merge.
