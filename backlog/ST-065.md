## Story
As a release manager, I want a release-planning skill, so that I can coordinate a releasable increment and verify what actually reached users.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Given accepted stories and a release goal, when the release-planning skill runs, then it writes a release manifest that separates planned, accepted, and released work, a rollout and recovery plan, and either actual delivery evidence or a specific handoff, and it invokes only approved deployment capabilities, verified by cargo test --test release_planning_skill on skills/release-planning/SKILL.md

## Out of Scope
Other lifecycle skills. Changing refinement, sprint-planning, or story-accept. Value scores and dates.
