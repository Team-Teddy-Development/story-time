## Story
As a backlog reader, I want story IDs in tracker titles, so that I can identify each story from its title.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Given a story with an ID and a title, when native story push creates or updates its tracker issue, then the title is ID: title with exactly one matching ID prefix and the local title remains unchanged.

## Out of Scope
Stored local titles, legacy Node exports, bulk renaming existing issues, and unrelated Rust conversion are excluded.
