## Story
As a story owner, I want story_accept and story lint to treat an indented sub-bullet under a checkbox criterion as part of that criterion, so that a story with a note under a criterion can be accepted.

Evidence (2026-11-26): ess-monorepo ESS-410 has `- [ ] Given ...` followed by `  - Note: ...` under criterion 5. story_accept returned `lifecycle_refused: Every criterion must have a checkbox.` Cause: `LIST_ITEM` in native/story-push/src/lint.rs:161 accepts leading whitespace, so `criteria` (lint.rs:235) counts the note as a 7th criterion without a checkbox, and `tick_criteria` (lint.rs:250) returns None. `story::acceptance_criteria` (story.rs:246) has the same rule and must agree.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given a story with six checkbox criteria where one criterion is followed by an indented `  - Note:` line, when story_accept runs with evidence for six criteria, then the story is accepted and lint, accept and check-pr all count 6 criteria.
