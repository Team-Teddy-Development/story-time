## Story
As a story-time team lead, I want a agile-metrics skill, so that I can read delivery metrics whose source, window, and units are named.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Given timestamped story state events for a window with one gap in history, when the agile-metrics skill runs, then every reported figure names its source, window, and units, the gap is reported as unknown instead of filled, and the figures come from a Rust tool, verified by cargo test --test agile_metrics_skill on skills/agile-metrics/SKILL.md

## Out of Scope
Other lifecycle skills. Changing refinement, sprint-planning, or story-accept. Value scores and dates.

## Notes
Source: ST-032 (epic EP-002, map backlog/EP-002.map.md). Slice 4: trusted reporting. Responsibility: docs/spikes/ST-019-atlassian-lifecycle.md (line 121). Needs timestamped state events, which do not exist yet (spike:196-197). The estimate includes the Rust calculation tool.

## Evidence
- Red 9a3146e: all 4 agile_metrics_contract tests failed (native had no `metrics` command), all 5 agile_metrics_skill tests failed (skills/agile-metrics/SKILL.md missing), and help_lists_native failed.
- Green 2ec7d77: native/story-push/src/metrics.rs adds read-only `story metrics --from YYYY-MM-DD --to YYYY-MM-DD [--json]`. It reads only changelog `set ... status=` lines at day resolution and reports throughput (stories), WIP at window end (stories), cycle time (days, median plus per story), and velocity (always `unknown`, no sprint record is stored). Every figure carries metric, value, units, window, source, definition, and unknown. A story whose status has no matching event is a history gap and is listed as `unknown` with its reason, never filled. skills/agile-metrics/SKILL.md tells the agent to run that command, present its figures as given, and never fill gaps.
