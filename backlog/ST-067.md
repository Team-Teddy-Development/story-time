## Story
As a story-time team lead, I want a agile-metrics skill, so that I can read delivery metrics whose source, window, and units are named.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given timestamped story state events for a window with one gap in history, when the agile-metrics skill runs, then every reported figure names its source, window, and units, the gap is reported as unknown instead of filled, and the figures come from a Rust tool, verified by cargo test --test agile_metrics_skill on skills/agile-metrics/SKILL.md

## Out of Scope
Other lifecycle skills. Changing refinement, sprint-planning, or story-accept. Value scores and dates.
