## Story
As a story-time team, I want a retrospective skill, so that I can turn recurring delivery problems into a few owned, measured improvement actions.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given previous improvement actions and flow evidence, when the retrospective skill runs, then it revisits each previous action first and then proposes at most three owned actions, each with the observed problem, proposed change, expected effect, follow-up date, and outcome measure, and it changes no policy, verified by cargo test --test retrospective_skill on skills/retrospective/SKILL.md

## Out of Scope
Other lifecycle skills. Changing refinement, sprint-planning, or story-accept. Value scores and dates.
