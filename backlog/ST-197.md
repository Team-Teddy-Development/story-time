## Story
As a story-time user working through an agent, I want to ask "what's our velocity", "show the burndown" or "when will this epic be done" and get the real figures, so that planning conversations use data rather than guesses.

## Source task
2026-11-25, velocity and burndown design (PR #444). Depends on ST-194, plus the burndown and forecast stories.

## To plan
- The hosted MCP server exposes `story_velocity`, `story_burndown` and `story_forecast` read-only, with the same JSON as the CLI. `story_list` filters by `sprint`, and `story_update` sets `sprint`.
- The `agile-metrics` skill reports all three. `sprint-planning` sizes the commitment from the velocity range and sets `sprint` on the chosen stories. `sprint-review` shows the burndown and committed against done.
- The `story-time` router sends "burndown", "velocity", "forecast" and "when will it be done" to these.
- `story burndown --svg`: a small static chart drawn by hand in Rust, for PR bodies and reports.
