## Story
As a team lead reading the delivery dashboard, I want its velocity, burndown and forecast panels, and a new estimated-against-shipped panel, drawn from story-time's own figures, so that the dashboard and the CLI give one answer and the forecast shows a 50% and an 85% date instead of a single date at an average pace.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given the JSON of story burndown, story forecast and story estimates for a repository, when the dashboard loads them as datasets, then its forecast panel shows the 50% and 85% dates from story forecast for that repository
