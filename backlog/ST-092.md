## Story
As a maintainer, I want story split to skip IDs that another branch already uses, as story new does, so that two branches that split stories never create the same IDs.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given a repository whose other branch holds ST-200 and a local backlog that ends at ST-090, when story split creates two children, then their IDs are above ST-200, verified by a native/story-push contract test

## Notes
Source: ST-061 (PR #186) real-data run. Node splitStory and the native port allocate child IDs only from local frontmatter IDs and ignore gitReservedIds, unlike story new. With ST-200 on another branch, both gave ST-088. ST-061 kept Node behavior as its Out of Scope required.
