## Story
As a team lead, I want the completion forecast to sample how long stories of each size actually took for the same builder and repository, so that the 50% and 85% dates stop repeating the same estimation bias every sprint.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given a repository whose story estimates --json reports a calibrated group for every remaining size, when story forecast --ids runs with --calibrated and a fixed seed, then the JSON reports sample_source calibrated and the same 50% and 85% dates on every run
- [ ] Given a remaining story whose size has no group with at least 10 closed stories, when story forecast --calibrated runs, then that story is sampled from raw velocity and is listed under uncalibrated with the reason from story estimates
