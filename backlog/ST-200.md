## Story
As a team lead running a sprint review or retrospective, I want each finished story's estimate shown against how long it took, with the largest misses first, so that the team talks about the estimates that went wrong rather than guessing which ones did.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given a closed sprint whose done stories have estimate and actual rows in story estimates --json, when the sprint-review skill reports the sprint, then it lists each story's estimate, actual days and the group median, ordered by the ratio of actual to group median, largest first
