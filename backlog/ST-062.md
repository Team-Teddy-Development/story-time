## Story
As a story-time team lead, I want a flow-review skill, so that I can see blocked, aging, and over-WIP work with an owner and next action for each.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given an active sprint with a blocked story, a story older than the aging limit, and WIP over its limit, when the flow-review skill runs, then it reports each item with an owner and a next action, records any scope swap with its capacity effect, and changes no story status or acceptance, verified by cargo test --test flow_review_skill on skills/flow-review/SKILL.md

## Out of Scope
Other lifecycle skills. Changing refinement, sprint-planning, or story-accept. Value scores and dates.
