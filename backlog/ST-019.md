## Findings
Answer: extend the existing story lifecycle with eight new coordination skills and one replacement refinement entry point. Preserve fixed sprints, continuous product-backlog prioritization, WIP-limited pull, and release independence. Reuse existing story-map epic ownership and story-level edit/estimate/readiness/acceptance skills.

Research: docs/spikes/ST-019-atlassian-lifecycle.md. Complete 75-page findings: docs/spikes/ST-019-atlassian-articles.md. Source manifest: docs/spikes/ST-019-source-manifest.tsv. Four supplemental Jira support pages establish ranking/epic/sprint/version distinctions, explicit active-sprint scope changes, incomplete-work disposition, and independent epic completion.

Follow-up: ST-018 owns the initial refinement and sprint-planning implementation. The research map specifies later flow review, sprint review, release coordination, retrospective, discovery, metrics, and status responsibilities as proposed slices, not additional implemented features. Detailed sizing remains unestimated until contracts and adapter decisions are refined; no value scores or dates are invented.
