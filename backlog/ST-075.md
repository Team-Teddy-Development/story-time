## Story
As a team lead, I want story metrics to read split into changelog lines as a split status event, so that split stories are not reported as history gaps.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given a story whose changelog has a split into line and whose status is split, when story metrics runs over a window that includes that date, then the story has no history gap and is not listed as unknown, verified by a case in native/story-push/tests/agile_metrics_contract.rs
