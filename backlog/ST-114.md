## Story
As an agent filing stories in parallel, I want story new to never overwrite a story another process just created, so that no story is silently lost.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Given two story new processes that allocate the same id, when both save, then both stories survive under distinct ids, or the second fails with an error and the first file is unchanged, verified by a native/story-push test that races two creates

## Notes
Source: ST-006 spike (docs/spikes/ST-006-mcp-server.md), found by reading native/story-push/src/new_story.rs: it takes next_id and then list::save renames a temp file over <id>.md, so two concurrent creates can get the same id and the second replaces the first. Reproduced by the coordinator on 2026-11-14 with the installed story (release 6468a12-era): 50 pairs of concurrent story new runs in a files-tracker temp repo left 99 story files and 99 distinct titles out of 100 creates, so one story was silently replaced. Prerequisite for ST-113.
